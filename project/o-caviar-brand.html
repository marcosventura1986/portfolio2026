Ó Caviar — Brand & Packaging

Client Ó Caviar (Premium Delicatessen)
Year 2024
Pillar Graphic
Duration 4 weeks

30s Summary

  • Challenge: Create a luxurious, modern brand that honours Irish heritage while elevating the premium nature of the caviar product.
  • Approach: A logo built from a perfect circular brush-stroke, paired with refined typography, Ogham-inspired detailing, a deep green-almost-black palette, and gold accents.
  • Outcome: A cohesive brand system across logo, website, labels, and guidelines that feels authentically Irish, premium, and contemporary.

Design Partnership

This project was developed in close collaboration with the client, with a shared goal: to craft a luxurious brand for Ó Caviar that would reflect Irish heritage while elevating the premium quality of the product. From early research and mood boards to multiple iterations of the logo, the process was highly intentional, ensuring every detail aligned with the client’s vision and the authenticity of the Irish landscape.

Brand Identity & Logo Development

The logo was built around a perfect circular form, inspired by a hand-drawn brush stroke. This mark evokes the shape of a caviar pearl while also resembling a crescent moon, adding an elegant sense of movement and symbolism. Paired with clean, refined typography, the result is a signature identity that feels modern, premium, and rooted in craftsmanship.

Heritage-Driven Visual Language

The brand’s palette combines a deep black-green—referencing Ireland’s natural tones—with copper and gold accents to communicate luxury. Marble and linen textures were integrated to capture the richness of the Irish landscape while enhancing the tactile, premium feel of the brand. Ogham-inspired markings were introduced as a distinctive cultural detail, elevating the visual identity without resorting to clichés.

Website, Guidelines & Labels

I created the website, full brand guidelines, and the complete product label system, ensuring consistency across digital and physical applications. The labels unify all core elements—circular mark, fine typography, gold detailing, and Irish-rooted colour palette—to deliver packaging that is unmistakably premium and deeply connected to its heritage.

35% Sales Growth
