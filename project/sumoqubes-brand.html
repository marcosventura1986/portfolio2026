SumoQubes — IP Branding

Client SumoQubes (Gaming IP)
Year 2024
Pillar Graphic

30s Summary

  • Challenge: Create a flexible brand system that works across games, merchandise, and social media.
  • Approach: Developed a modular logo system with multiple lockups, vibrant color palette, and template library.
  • Outcome: Cohesive IP identity ready for multi-platform deployment with strong recognition potential.

IP Development

SumoQubes required a brand identity that could scale from app icons to billboards while maintaining the playful personality of the characters.

Logo System

We created a comprehensive logo suite including primary wordmark with character integration, icon-only version for app stores, and horizontal and stacked layouts.

Impact

+120% Social Engagement
