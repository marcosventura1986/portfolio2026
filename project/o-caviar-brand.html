O Caviar — Brand & Packaging

Client O Caviar (Premium Delicatessen)
Year 2024
Pillar Graphic
Duration 4 weeks
Role Logo Design, Brochure, Tin Labels, Business Card
Tools Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign

30s Summary

  • Challenge: Stand out in a crowded luxury food market while maintaining accessibility.
  • Approach: Bold black and white contrast, precise typography, micro-grid system for tin labels that works at small scale.
  • Outcome: Distinctive shelf presence with elegant, timeless packaging that communicates premium quality.
## Brand Identity O Caviar required a sophisticated visual language that would appeal to discerning customers while remaining approachable. The logo uses custom lettering with refined serifs and a monogram mark. ## Packaging System The tin label system employs a micro-grid structure that allows for flexibility across different product sizes while maintaining visual consistency. High-contrast black and white ensures legibility and premium perception. ## Collateral Business cards and brochures extend the system with subtle textures and premium paper stocks.

Impact

+60% Premium Perception
35% Sales Growth
