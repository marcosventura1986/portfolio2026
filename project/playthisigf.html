Year 2026
Pillar Web

30s Summary

  • Challenge: Create excitement and energy for a gaming event while maintaining accessibility and performance across devices.
  • Approach: Vibrant color palette; smooth scroll animations; interactive game showcase; modular sections for easy updates; reduced-motion support.
  • Outcome: High-energy site that generated strong pre-event buzz and served as the central hub for attendees.

Event Identity

Translated the PlayThis IGF brand into a dynamic web experience with bold typography, bright accent colors, and playful interactions.
