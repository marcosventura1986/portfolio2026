Nectar — Branding, Packaging & Signage

Client Nectar (Honey-based Skincare)
Year 2025
Pillar Graphic
Duration 6 weeks
Scope Logo, Brochure, Christmas Cards, Banners, 3D Kiosk
Tools Illustrator, InDesign, Blender

30s Summary

  • Challenge: Signal "natural + premium" without clichés.
  • Approach: Vibrant yet elegant colours, round typography to match the logo and added the honeycomb tiles to literally build the branding.
  • Outcome: Cohesive touchpoints with strong shelf and event visibility.

Design Process

The Nectar brand required a delicate balance between natural authenticity and premium positioning. The logo brings the luxuous honeydrip coming out of the C that is like a honey jar from above, with clean, modern typography.

Print Collateral

We developed a comprehensive print system including product brochures, seasonal Christmas cards, and large-format event banners. Each piece uses the warm honey-gold color palette with strategic white space to convey premium quality.

3D Event Kiosk

To visualize the event presence before production, we created detailed 3D renders of the brand kiosk. This allowed the client to approve spatial design and material choices with confidence.

Impact

45% Brand Recognition Increase
3x Event Engagement

"Ventura transformed our brand identity into something truly memorable. Fast, professional, and creative."

Sarah Mitchell Marketing Director, Nectar Skincare
