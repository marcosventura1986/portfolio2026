SumoQubes — IP Branding

Client SumoQubes (Gaming IP)
Year 2024
Pillar Graphic
Duration 8 weeks
Role IP Logo Suite, Key Art, Social Templates, Brand Guidelines
Tools Illustrator, Photoshop, After Effects

30s Summary

  • Challenge: Create a flexible brand system that works across games, merchandise, and social media while maintaining character personality.
  • Approach: Developed a modular logo system with multiple lockups, vibrant color palette, and template library for consistent social presence.
  • Outcome: Cohesive IP identity ready for multi-platform deployment with strong recognition and merchandising potential.
## IP Development SumoQubes required a brand identity that could scale from app icons to billboards while maintaining the playful personality of the characters. ## Logo System We created a comprehensive logo suite including: - Primary wordmark with character integration - Icon-only version for app stores - Horizontal and stacked layouts - Monochrome alternatives ## Key Art & Templates Developed hero key art and a library of social media templates that maintain brand consistency while allowing creative flexibility for campaigns.

Impact

+120% Social Engagement
85% Brand Recognition
